ONE PHONE, TWO BOUNDARIES

A work line that knows when you are off

Built for freelancers, tradespeople, consultants and small-business owners who use a private and work SIM in the same Android phone.

01

Separate ringtones

Hear which SIM is ringing before you look at your phone.

02

Working hours

Set one or more opening windows for each day of the week.

03

Closed means closed

Outside your schedule, work calls are rejected to your carrier voicemail.

04

Quick overrides

Open temporarily or close for the rest of the day without changing your schedule.

05

Fail-safe by design

If LinePilot cannot identify the incoming SIM with confidence, it allows the call.

06

Privacy first

No account, no advertising SDK and no automatic upload of calls or contacts.

SET UP IN MINUTES

Keep Google Phone. Add LinePilot.

  1. 1

    Choose your work SIM

    Your private line is never governed by the work schedule.

  2. 2

    Set your working hours

    Choose the days and times when customers may reach you.

  3. 3

    Let LinePilot handle the boundary

    Calls ring while open and go to carrier voicemail while closed.
