Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 August 2026 · Last updated: 23 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LinePilot handles personal data in the LinePilot Android application and on the LinePilot website. LinePilot is designed as a local-first service: its core call-management features operate on your device, without an app account or LinePilot-operated cloud service.

1. Scope and controller

This policy applies to the LinePilot Android app, linepilotapp.com, support communications and diagnostics that you choose to send. LinePilot is the data controller for personal data received directly by LinePilot. Privacy requests and questions can be sent to info@linepilotapp.com.

Android, Google Play, your email provider, your mobile carrier and GitHub may process data independently under their own privacy terms when you use their services.

2. Summary of our practices

  • No LinePilot account is required.
  • Core SIM, schedule, call-screening and ringtone data stays on your device.
  • The Android app has no advertising or analytics SDK and does not request internet access.
  • The website uses Google Analytics only after you actively accept optional analytics. If you decline, the Google Analytics tag is not loaded and no analytics data is sent.
  • LinePilot does not sell personal data, share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, or use it for targeted advertising.
  • Diagnostics leave your device only when you review and send them using an email app.

3. Data processed locally on your device

To provide the features you request, LinePilot processes the following information within Android app storage:

  • SIM and subscription data: active subscription identifier, SIM slot, carrier and display name, used to identify the work SIM reliably.
  • Incoming call state: whether a call is incoming and which subscription is ringing, used to apply your schedule and fail-safe rules.
  • Preferences: work-SIM selection, schedules, temporary overrides, ringtone references, language, onboarding state and feature settings.
  • Local technical status: permission and role state, the result of recent SIM detection, screening, ringtone and rejection operations, and timestamps used for health checks.
  • Contacts permission: requested so Android can send calls from saved contacts through the call-screening service where the platform requires it. LinePilot does not copy, build a contact database, or upload your address book.

LinePilot does not record call audio, voicemail or message content and does not request access to your telephone number or precise location.

4. Permissions and Android services

  • Phone status: identifies active SIM subscriptions and incoming call state.
  • Call Screening role: allows or rejects calls according to your settings; Google Phone remains the default dialler.
  • Contacts: provides the screening coverage described above.
  • Notifications and foreground media playback: support custom ringtone playback.
  • Start after reboot: restores the app's locally stored operating state after restart or update.

You may revoke optional permissions in Android settings. Some features will stop working if a permission required for that feature is removed. If LinePilot cannot identify an incoming SIM reliably, its fail-safe rule allows the call.

5. Diagnostics and support communications

LinePilot can prepare a diagnostic report containing the app version, device manufacturer and model, Android version, permission and role status, schedule and override state, and local technical success or error messages. The report is designed not to include telephone numbers, contact names, call audio, voicemail, message content or precise location.

Nothing is sent automatically. After an in-app confirmation, LinePilot opens your email app with a draft that you can review, edit, address differently or cancel. If you send it to LinePilot, we receive the report, your email address and anything else you include. We use this information to answer your request, troubleshoot compatibility and improve reliability.

6. Website data and optional analytics

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request data such as IP address, browser or device information, requested URL and timestamps to deliver, secure and maintain the hosting service under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

With your consent, the website loads Google Analytics 4 using measurement ID G-V9FW2FNCJP. It helps us understand whether the site is useful and whether visitors use the links to Google Play. Depending on your browser and interaction, Google Analytics may process:

  • pages viewed, page title, referring page, date and time, language and approximate session duration;
  • interactions such as scrolls and outbound link clicks, including the destination of a Google Play link;
  • browser, device category, operating system and approximate geographic area derived from the network connection; and
  • pseudonymous analytics identifiers stored in first-party cookies such as _ga and _ga_<container-id>.

We do not intentionally send names, email addresses, telephone numbers, contact data or app call information to Google Analytics. Google Signals and advertising-personalisation features are disabled. The website uses basic consent mode: the Google tag is completely blocked until you choose “Accept analytics”. If you decline, no consent ping or analytics measurement is sent to Google. Your choice is stored locally in your browser so the site can remember it. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time using the Analytics preferences link in the footer. Withdrawing consent removes the site's accessible Google Analytics cookies and stops the tag after the page reloads.

Google provides the service under its applicable terms and privacy information. You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

7. Purposes and legal bases

Where the GDPR, UK GDPR or similar laws require a legal basis, LinePilot relies on:

  • Performance of a contract or steps at your request: providing the app functions and support you request.
  • Legitimate interests: securing, maintaining and diagnosing LinePilot, responding to communications and preventing misuse, balanced against your rights and expectations.
  • Consent: measuring website visits and Google Play link clicks with Google Analytics, and processing optional diagnostic information that you deliberately send. You may withdraw consent for future processing at any time.
  • Legal obligation: retaining or disclosing limited information when applicable law requires it.

Providing local permissions and settings is optional, but LinePilot cannot perform the related feature without them. Sending diagnostics and accepting website analytics are always optional.

8. Disclosure and service providers

LinePilot does not disclose on-device app data to analytics providers or other third parties. Personal data that you deliberately send may be processed by our email provider and other providers necessary to respond securely. With website analytics consent, Google processes the website data described in section 6 to provide Google Analytics. Google also independently processes app distribution and billing information under its own terms. GitHub processes website-hosting data. Mobile carriers independently handle calls and voicemail. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, investigate abuse, or complete a business reorganisation subject to appropriate safeguards and notice.

9. International transfers

Email, website-hosting, Google Analytics and platform providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where LinePilot is responsible for a restricted international transfer, we use a legally recognised mechanism where required, such as an adequacy decision, standard contractual clauses or another valid safeguard. Google's relevant data-transfer safeguards are described in its contractual and privacy documentation. You may contact us for information about applicable safeguards.

10. Retention and deletion

  • On-device data: retained until you change or clear it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall LinePilot, subject to Android backup and restore settings controlled by you or Google.
  • Google Analytics: event-level and user-level website analytics are configured for deletion after 14 months. Aggregated reports may remain available for longer. Google Analytics first-party cookies may remain for up to two years unless you withdraw consent, clear browser data or your browser removes them sooner.
  • Support and diagnostic emails: retained only while needed to resolve the request, analyse reliability and maintain reasonable support records, normally no longer than 24 months after the last relevant communication, unless a shorter period is appropriate or law requires longer retention.
  • Legal and security records: retained only for the period reasonably necessary to meet the applicable obligation or establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

You can delete local LinePilot data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. You can stop future website analytics through Analytics preferences and clear existing website data through your browser controls. To request deletion of information sent to us, email info@linepilotapp.com.

11. Security

LinePilot minimises data collection and uses Android's permission model and application sandbox for local data. Access to received support information is limited to people who need it for the purposes described above. We use reasonable organisational and technical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the data. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

12. Automated decisions

LinePilot automatically allows, silences or rejects an incoming call according to the work SIM, schedule and overrides you configure. This rule is executed locally, is controlled by you, does not profile callers and does not produce legal or similarly significant effects under data-protection law. If SIM identification is uncertain, the call is allowed.

13. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and subject to legal exceptions, you may have rights to know or access personal data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to processing, receive portable data, withdraw consent, and appeal or complain about our response. You may also have the right not to receive discriminatory treatment for exercising privacy rights.

Most app data is available only on your device and cannot be accessed by LinePilot. You control it through the app and Android settings. For data sent to us, submit a request to info@linepilotapp.com. We may verify your identity proportionately before fulfilling a request. Where legally permitted, an authorised agent may submit a request with proof of authority.

14. Regional disclosures

EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland

You may withdraw analytics consent at any time, object to processing based on legitimate interests and lodge a complaint with the data-protection authority where you live or work, or where you believe an infringement occurred. You may also contact us first so we can try to resolve the concern.

California and other US states

In the preceding 12 months, the categories of personal information LinePilot may have processed were identifiers, Internet or electronic network activity, approximate location, and device or technical information from consent-based website analytics or support communications. The sources, purposes, recipients and retention rules are described above. LinePilot does not sell personal information, does not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, does not use or disclose sensitive personal information to infer characteristics, and has no actual knowledge that it sells or shares personal information of consumers under 16. Because no sale, sharing or targeted advertising occurs, there is no sale/sharing opt-out link or Global Privacy Control signal to apply. Analytics remains optional through the site's consent controls. Where applicable, residents may request access, correction, deletion, portability and appeal, and may use an authorised agent.

Brazil

Subject to the LGPD, you may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymisation, blocking or deletion, portability where regulated, information about sharing, withdrawal of consent, review of qualifying automated decisions, and information about the consequences of withholding consent. You may complain to the Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados after contacting us.

Other regions

Residents of Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, South Korea and other jurisdictions may exercise applicable access, correction, deletion, objection, consent-withdrawal and complaint rights by contacting us. Mandatory local rights are not limited by this policy.

15. Children

LinePilot is designed for adults managing a work telephone line and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent personal data to us, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where required.

16. Carrier, Google Play and purchases

When LinePilot rejects a call, your carrier may route it to carrier voicemail under your subscription and carrier settings. LinePilot does not operate or store voicemail. LinePilot Pro purchases are processed through Google Play Billing under Google's Privacy Policy; LinePilot does not receive full payment-card details. Google Play supplies the app with purchase entitlement information so it can provide the purchased features.

17. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when LinePilot, legal requirements or data practices change. We will change the date above and provide additional notice where required for a material change. Earlier versions may be requested by email.

18. Contact and complaints

Email: info@linepilotapp.com

Please include enough information for us to understand your request. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to your local privacy or data-protection authority where the law gives you that right.
